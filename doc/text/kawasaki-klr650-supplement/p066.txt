Caliper Assembly Points 
•Apply brake fluid to the outside of the piston and the fluid 
seal, and push the piston into the cylinder by hand. Take 
care that neither the cylinder nor the piston skirt scratched. 
•Apply a thin coat of PBC (Poly Butyl Cuprysil) grease to 
the caliper holder shafts and holder holes. (PBC is a special 
high temperature, water-resistant grease). 
•Install the anti-rattle spring in the caliper as shown.

A. Anti-rattle Spring
