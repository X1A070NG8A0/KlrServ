Mechanical and Oil Seal Replacement

The water pump has a two-piece mechanical seal 
(A, Figure 15) and an oil seal (B). The mechanical

seal prevents coolant in the pump chamber from 
passing into the right crankcase cover, which con-

tains oil. Likewise, the oil seal prevents oil in the

right crankcase cover from passing into the pump 
chamber, which contains coolant. A drain hole (Fig-

ure 11) is located between the seals to allow any 
water or oil leakage to drain to the outside of the en-

gine. Whenever there is leakage at the drain hole, 
replace the seals. The mechanical seal must be re- 
moved from the crankcase cover in order to remove

the oil seal.

1. Replace the mechanical seal in the impeller as 
follows:

a. Lift the impeller seal from the impeller (Fig-

ure 16). Clean the seal bore.

b. Lightly lubricate the rubber edge of the new

seal with waterproof grease.

c. Seat the new seal into the impeller by hand.

2. In the crankcase cover, replace the remaining

half of the mechanical seal and the oil seal as fol- 
lows:

a. Place a narrow drift under the oil seal and

onto the back of the mechanical seal (Figure

17). Work around the seal and drive it from

the bore. Avoid any contact with the surface 
of the bore. Do not pry the seal from the front

side of the crankcase cover.
