STARTER

Removal and Installation

Refer to Figure 22.

1. Remove the exhaust pipe (Chapter Four).

2. Disconnect the ground (negative) cable from the 
battery and from the starter (A, Figure 23).

3. Disconnect the positive cable from the starter (B, 
Figure 23).

4. Remove the remaining mounting bolt, then twist

the starter out of the left crankcase cover. The starter

is sealed to the cover by an O-ring, which causes re-

sistance during removal.

5. Disassemble, inspect and test the starter as de- 
scribed in this section.

6. Reverse this procedure to install the starter. Note

the following:

a. Lubricate the O-ring on the front end cover
