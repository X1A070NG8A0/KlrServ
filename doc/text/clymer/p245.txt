REAR WHEEL

Removal and Installation

1. Support the motorcycle so it is stable and the rear 
wheel is off the ground.

2. Remove the two bolts securing the brake caliper

to the caliper holder (Figure 13).

NOTE 
Do not operate the rear brake pedal 
while the wheel is removed. Insert a 
wooden block between the pads until

the wheel is installed. This prevents

the caliper piston from extending if 
operating the pedal.

3. Remove the cotter pin, nut and washer from the 
axle (Figure 14).

4. Pull up on the rear wheel to take the weight off

the axle, then remove the axle.

5. Lower the wheel and remove the chain from the

sprocket. Roll the wheel out of the swing arm.

6. Remove the spacer from the right side (Figure

15) and left side of the hub (Figure 16).
