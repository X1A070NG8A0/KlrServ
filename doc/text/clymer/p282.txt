SHOCK ABSORBER LINKAGE

The shock linkage consists of the lever, lever 
arms, pivot bolts, seals, pivot spacers and needle 
bearings (Figure 13 and Figure 14). The lever is 
joined to the swing arm by the lever arms. The link-

age should be disassembled and lubricated at the in-

tervals indicated in Chapter Three. If the linkage is

often subjected to harsh riding conditions, service

the linkage more frequently.
