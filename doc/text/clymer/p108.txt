VALVES

Solvent Test

Perform the solvent test with the valve assembly

in the cylinder head. The test can reveal if valves are

fully seating, as well as expose undetected cracks in

the cylinder head.

1. Remove the cylinder head as described in this

chapter.

2. Check that the combustion chamber is dry and

the valves are seated.

3. Support the cylinder head so the port faces up 
(Figure 52).

4. Pour solvent or kerosene into the port.

5. Inspect the combustion chamber for leakage 
around the valve.

6. Repeat Steps 3-5 for the other valves.

7. If there is leakage, it can be caused by:

a. A worn or damaged valve face.

b. A worn or damaged valve seat (in the cylinder

head).

c. A bent valve stem.

d. A crack in the combustion chamber.
