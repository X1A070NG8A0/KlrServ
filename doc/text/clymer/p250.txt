6. Before installing the new bearings, note the fol- 
lowing:

a. Inspect the new bearings and determine

which side faces out. This is usually the side 
with the manufacturer’s marks and numbers. 
Bearings that are sealed on one side should be

installed with the sealed side facing out.

. Apply waterproof grease to bearings that are

not lubricated by the manufacturer, or that are

not sealed on both sides. Work the grease into

the cavities between the balls and races.

. Always support the bottom side of the hub,

near the bore, when installing bearings.

. To aid in driving the bearings, chill them in a
