All Models

Before beginning assembly, have new snap rings

on hand. Throughout the procedure, the orientation 
of many parts is made in relation to first gear (Fig-

ure 10), on the input shaft. If desired, lock the lower 
portion of the shaft (below first gear) in a padded

vise. With the shaft held stable and vertical, installa-

tion of 
the snap rings will be easier. Do not allow the

vise to damage the shaft.
