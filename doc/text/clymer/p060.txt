5. Remove the filter and mounting pin (Figure 10).

6. Clean and inspect the filter housing and parts.

a. The oil filter bypass valve is inside the

mounting pin (A, Figure 11). When cleaning

the pin, check that the spring-loaded valve is 
clean and free to open. Press the valve open 
from the narrow (stepped) end of the mount- 
ing pin.

b. Install a new seal washer on the drain plug.

Replace the drain plug if the threads are dam- 
aged or excessively worn.

c. Install a new, lubricated O-ring onto the filter

cover (B, Figure 11).

d. If leakage is evident around the oil fill plug,

replace the O-ring on the plug.

7. Wipe dirt and oil from around the drain plug

hole, then install and torque the drain plug to 23 
Nem (17 ft.-lb.).
