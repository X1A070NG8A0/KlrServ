3. To install the upper race, do the following:

b. Assemble the tool, seating a driver at the

outer edge of the race. The lower driver can

c. Hold the lower nut with a wrench and tighten

the upper nut to seat the race.

d. Remove the tool from the frame and check

e. Repeat the procedure to install the second

race. During installation, do not allow the 
driver or shaft to contact the face of the first

race. Damage will occur.

4. Lubricate the races with waterproof bearing 
grease.

Steering Stem Bearing Replacement

The steering stem bearing is press-fitted into

place. Perform the following steps to replace the 
bearing.

1. Thread the stem nut (Figure 15) onto the steer- 
ing stem to protect the threads.

2. Stabilize the steering stem, then remove the 
bearing and seal, using a hammer and chisel (Fig-

ure 15). To prevent binding, make several passes 
around the perimeter of the bearing.

WARNING

Wear safety glasses when using the 
hammer and chisel.

3. Clean and inspect the steering stem.
