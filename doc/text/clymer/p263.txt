STEERING STEM AND HEAD

The steering stem pivots in the steering head on 
tapered roller bearings. The bearings are at the top 
and bottom of the steering stem. Do not remove the

inner races (mounted in the frame) and the lower 
bearing (mounted on the steering stem) unless they

Disassembly

1. Remove the front wheel (Chapter Eleven).

2. Remove the bolts securing the front fender (Fig-

ure 6).

3. Remove the fuel tank, radiator covers and fairing 
(Chapter Fifteen).

4. Remove the handlebar as described in this chap-

ter.

5. Loosen the stem nut (A, Figure 7).

6. Remove the fork legs as described in this chap-

ter.

7. Remove the gauges and ignition switch from the 
upper fork bridge.

8 Remove the clamps from the left side of the up-
