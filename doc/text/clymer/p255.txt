SPROCKETS

Check the condition of both sprockets and the

drive chain, as described in Chapter Three. If either

the chain or sprockets are worn, replace all drive 
components. Using new sprockets with a worn 
chain, or a new chain on worn sprockets will 
shorten the life of the new part.
