CAM CHAIN AND LOWER GUIDE

If the engine balancer assembly will be removed,

the cam chain and lower guide can be removed dur- 
ing that procedure. Use the following procedure 
when only the cam chain and lower guide need to be 
removed.

Removal, Inspection and Installation

1. Remove the cam shafts, then remove the cam 
chain from the camshaft sprockets (Chapter Four).

2. Remove the alternator cover and rotor (Chapter 
Nine).

3. Remove the cam chain guide and the balancer 
chain guide (Figure 5). If necessary, rotate the 
crankshaft to access the bolt behind the front left 
balancer weight.

4. Remove the cam chain from the sprocket.

5. Inspect the cam chain and guide (Figure 6).
