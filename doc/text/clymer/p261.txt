CHAPTER TWELVE

FRONT SUSPENSION AND STEERING

This chapter provides service procedures for the

front suspension and steering components. This in-

cludes the handlebar, steering head and fork. Refer

to the tables at the end of the chapter for specifica-

tions, capacities and torque requirements.

HANDLEBAR

Removal and Installation

Use the following procedure to remove and in-

stall the handlebar into the holders. If the handlebar 
only needs to be repositioned, adjust by loosening

holders.

5. Clean the handlebar and holders.

6. Reverse this procedure to install the handlebar.

Note the following:

a. Position the holder caps so the arrows on the
