ROTOR AND STARTER CLUTCH

The starter clutch is mounted on the back of the

rotor. The starter gears and rotor must be removed

to access the clutch. To remove the rotor, hold it

with a rotor holder tool, then remove it with a rotor

puller. The tools shown in the following procedure

are 
the 
Kawasaki 
rotor 
holder 
(part 
No.

57001-1184) 
and 
rotor 
puller 
(part 
No.

57001-1185).

NOTE 
If troubleshooting the starter clutch,

the clutch can be checked for free- 
wheel and lockup without removing 
the rotor. Turn the clutch gear clock- 
wise (Figure 
9). The clutch gear 
should turn freely and smoothly in

that direction. Attempt to turn the 
gear 
counterclockwise. 
The 
gear 
should not turn. If the gear turns in

both directions or is always locked up, 
disassemble and inspect the clutch as- 
sembly.
