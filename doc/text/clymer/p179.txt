TRANSMISSION INSPECTION

Use the following procedures to inspect the shaft 
assemblies for all models. Because of component 
design changes, some inspections may not be re- 
quired for the assembly being serviced.

Shaft Inspection

1. Inspect the shafts for the following:

Broken or damaged gear teeth (A, Figure 38) 
on the input shaft.

a.

_ Worn or damaged splines (B, Figure 38).

c. Rounded or damaged snap ring grooves (C,

Figure 38).

_ Clean oil holes (A, Figure 39).

_ Wear, galling or other damage on the bear-

ing/bushing surfaces (B, Figure 39). A blue 
discoloration indicates excessive heat. 
Damaged threads (C, Figure 39). Mildly 
damaged threads can be trued with a thread

die.

2. Assemble the shafts as described in this chapter.
