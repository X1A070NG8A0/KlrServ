Heat range

Spark plugs are available in several heat ranges to

put on the engine. The standard spark plug recom- 
mended by manufacturers is usually a medium 
heat-range plug that operates well over a wide range 
of engine speeds. As long as engine speeds vary, 
these plugs will stay relatively clean and perform

well.

or under heavy loads for prolonged periods, a spark 
plug with a colder heat range is recommended. A
