299

REAR BRAKE CALIPER

Removal and Installation

Use the following procedure to remove the cali- 
per from the motorcycle. Refer to Figure 49.

1A. If the caliper will be disassembled, drain the 
system as described in this chapter. After draining,

loosen the brake hose banjo bolt (A, Figure 56) 
while the caliper is stable on the swing arm. Leave

the bolt finger-tight. It will be removed in a later

step.
