Piston-to-Cylinder Clearance Check

Determine the clearance between the piston and 
cylinder to determine if the parts can be reused. If

parts do not fall within specification, the cylinder 
should be bored oversize to match an oversize pis-

ton assembly. Clean and dry the piston and cylinder 
before measuring.

1. Measure the outside diameter of the piston. Mea-

sure 5 mm (0.2 in.) up from the bottom edge of the 
piston skirt and 90° to the direction of 
the piston pin

(Figure 89). Record the measurement.

2. Determine clearance by subtracting the piston 
measurement from the largest cylinder measure- 
ment. If cylinder measurements are not yet known,

the procedure is described under Cylinder Inspec-

tion in this chapter. If the clearance exceeds the 
specifications in Table 2, the cylinder must be

overbored and fitted with an oversize piston assem-

bly.
