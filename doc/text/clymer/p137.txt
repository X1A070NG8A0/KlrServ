CRANKCASE

The following procedures detail the disassembly

and reassembly of the crankcase. When the two

halves 
of the crankcase 
are 
disassembled—or

split-the crankshaft, balancer shafts and transmis-

sion assemblies can be removed for inspection and

repair. Before proceeding with this procedure, re-

move the engine from the frame as described in this

chapter. All assemblies located in the crankcase

covers must be removed. It may be easier to remove

these assemblies with the engine in the frame. The

engine will remain steady during the disassembly

process.
