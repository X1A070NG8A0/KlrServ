LEFT HANDLEBAR SWITCHES

Switch Tests

The left handlebar switch unit (Figure 75) houses

the headlight high/low switch, turn signal switch

and horn button. The switches can be checked for

continuity using an ohmmeter, connected to the ap-

propriate color-coded wires in the connector plug

(D, Figure 70). The plug is located in front of the

handlebar gauges, and behind the cover. Remove

the cover to access the wiring. If necessary, remove

the fairing.

Refer to the Continuity Testing Guidelines sec-

tion in this chapter for the test procedure.
