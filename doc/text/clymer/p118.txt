the piston pin. The hole in the pipe must be larger

end of the rod is tightened, the nut and washer at the 
opposite end drive the piston pin into the pipe.

CAUTION 
Do not attempt to drive the pin out

with a hammer and drift. The piston 
and connecting rod assembly will 
likely be damaged.
