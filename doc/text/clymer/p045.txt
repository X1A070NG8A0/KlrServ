3. Side stand switch. If the side stand is down with

Engine Is Cold

1. Shift the transmission into neutral.

2. Check that the engine stop switch is in the run

position.

3. Turn the fuel valve lever from the off position 
(Figure 3) to the on (vertical) position. An arrow on

the fuel valve points to the lever position.

4. Move the choke lever (Figure 4) fully to the left

to richen the air/fuel mixture.

5. Turn on the ignition switch (A, Figure 5).

6. Check that the neutral light (B, Figure 5) comes

on.

7. Press the starter button (B, Figure 2) while keep- 
ing the throttle closed.

8. When the engine starts, gradually move the

quire the choke.

CAUTION

warm-up period. Excessive wear and
