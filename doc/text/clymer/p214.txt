Starter Circuit Relay 
Testing

The starter circuit relay (B, Figure 40) is located

at the left side of the motorcycle, behind the plastic

cover. The starter circuit relay passes current to the

starter relay, which activates the starter. In order for

the starter circuit relay to pass current, it requires

that the starter lockout switch or neutral switch be in

its safe startup position. When either or both of 
these switches are in their safe positions, the switch

in the starter circuit relay closes and completes the

circuit. Refer to Figure 39.
