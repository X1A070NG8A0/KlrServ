9. Install the plates into the clutch housing and

clutch hub as follows:

a. Lubricate the plates with engine oil. To pre-

vent possible seizure, particularly with new

friction plates, it is important that the face of

the friction plates (Figure 40) be completely 
coated with oil. If possible, allow the plates to 
soak in engine oil before installing.

b. Beginning with a friction plate, alternately in-

stall friction plates and clutch plates into the

clutch housing and clutch hub (Figure 41).

NOTE
