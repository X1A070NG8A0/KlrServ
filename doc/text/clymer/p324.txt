Vacuum Bleeding

To vacuum-bleed the brake system, have a 10 mm 
wrench and a vacuum pump, such as the Mityvac 
pump shown in Figure 100. Use the following pro- 
cedure to bleed either the front or rear brake.

CAUTION 
Brake fluid can damage painted and 
finished surfaces. Use water and im-

mediately wash any surface that be- 
comes contaminated with brake fluid.

1. Check that the banjo bolts are tight at the master

cylinder and caliper.

2. Attach the brake bleeder to the bleeder valve 
(Figure 100). Suspend the tool with wire. This al-
