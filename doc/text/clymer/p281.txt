Inspection

Individual parts are available for repairing the 
shock absorber. If damper leakage is evident, re- 
place the complete shock absorber, as the damper is

not available separately and is not rebuildable. If 
disassembly of the shock absorber is required, it is 
recommended that it be disassembled by a dealer-

ship. Because of 
the design of 
the shock absorber, a 
common spring compressor will not work in remov- 
ing the spring.

or drive out the bushing. Install a new bushing and 
mounting bolt.
