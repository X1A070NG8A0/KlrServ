Regulator/Rectifier

The regulator/rectifier is located under the seat 
(Figure 60). The regulator/rectifier converts the al-

ternating current produced by the alternator into di-

rect current to charge the battery and power any

electrical loads (Figure 56). The unit also regulates

the charging voltage to the battery. Excess voltage is

dissipated as heat and radiated from the finned regu-

lator.
