OUTPUT SHAFT

Use the following procedures to disassemble and 
assemble the output shaft. The design of the output

shaft and gears changed significantly in 1996 (after 
engine serial number KL650AE032209). The early

shaft design is shouldered to the outside of second 
gear and all parts are removed from one end of the

shaft. The later shaft design is stepped to the inside 
of second gear and requires parts to be removed 
from both ends of the shaft. Figures of both output

shaft assemblies are provided.
