Inspect the pads for contamination, scoring

other chemicals. If the pads are worn un- 
evenly, the caliper is probably not sliding cor-

free to float on the pins. Buildup or corrosion 
on the pins can hold the caliper in one posi-

tion, causing brake drag and excessive pad 
wear.
