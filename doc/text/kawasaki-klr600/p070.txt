By circulating in the cooling system, the coolant transfers the heat generated by the engine to the radiator. To
protect the cooling system (consisting of the aluminum engine and radiator) from rust and corrosion, the use of
corrosion and rust inhibitor chemicals in the water is essential. If coolant containing corrosion and rust inhibitor
chemicals is not used, over a period of time the cooling system accumulates rust and scale in the water jacket and
radiator, This clogs up the water passages, and considerably reduces the efficiency of the cooling system.
