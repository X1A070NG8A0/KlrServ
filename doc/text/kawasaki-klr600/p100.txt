Bearing Lubrication

@Remove the wheel bearings from the wheel hub and
wheel coupling.

@Wash the bearings with a high flash-point solvent, dry
them (do not spin them while they are dry), and oil
them.

@Spin each bearing by hand to check its condition.

xIf it is noisy, does not spin smoothly, or has any rough
spots, it must be replaced.

elf the same bearing is to be used again, re-wash it with a
high flash-point solvent, and dry it.

@Pack each bearing with good quality bearing grease
before installation. Turn each bearing around by hand
a few times to make sure the grease is distributed
uniformly inside the bearing, and wipe the old grease
out of each bearing housing before bearing installation.
