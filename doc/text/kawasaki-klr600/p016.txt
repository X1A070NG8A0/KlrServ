Tighteness Inspection
eCheck the tightness of the bolts and nuts listed here.
Also, check to see that each cotter pin is in place and in

good condition.

“NOTE”

OFor the engine fasteners, check the tightness of them
when the engine is cold (at room temperature).

xlf there are loose fasteners, retorque them to the
specified torque following the specified tightening
sequence, For each fastener, first loosen it by 2 turn,

then tighten it.
xIf cotter pins are damaged, replace them with new ones.
