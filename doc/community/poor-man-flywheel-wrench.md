Re: Poor man flywheel wrench?


You may want to review the procedure. I'm not sure where you're going to brace that rotor holding setup using a crow's foot and an extension and a ratchet, without having something come apart and drop a 1/2 ratchet on your foot.


By using a 1 piece wrench, you utilize the foot peg to hold the rotor in place while you use the puller.


Have you reached out to others to see if there's someone local with the proper tool that you can borrow?


TBH, I don't see why it wouldn't work... but I feel a 1 pc wrench would be much easier, and less prone to flying apart on you.


Not to mention, when it comes time to torque the new rotor bolt down... that's a lot of foot pounds of torque... It's not the easiest thing to do, it takes both hands and some weight behind the TQ wrench to get to the final TQ specs, and THAT'S when I see the extension popping out of the crow's foot...

Re: Poor man flywheel wrench?


I bought a combo wrench on amazon and then cut/welded it. I don't have an acetylene torch, but I have a beefy stick welder. It may not look pretty, but it gets the job done.


Like Robert said, it takes a lot of torque to undo and redo that bolt. piecing together ratchets is likely to end up just falling apart as the torque to assemble it will exceed the torque at which your three piece crowfoot/adapter/ratchet will fall apart. It could also result in rounding off the edges of the rotor, which is a big deal.
