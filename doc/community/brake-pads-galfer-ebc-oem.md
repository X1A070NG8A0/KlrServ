Re: Brake Pads? Galfer, EBC or OEM?


Galfer Green are softer than Galfer Black which are softer than OEM. The softer the pad, the greater the stopping capacity at the expense of longevity. I've been going with the OEM for a longer lasting pad. I don't need that extra stopping power.

They say that the hard pads wear the rotor more, but I have not verified this.

I vote to ban the word(is it really a color?) Fuschia.

Re: Brake Pads? Galfer, EBC or OEM?


I'd go with OEM also- I've never heard of anyone having any problems with them, other than maybe the cost.

I put EBC sintered pads on when I installed my new 320 rotor, and the braking effectiveness and resonant noise was actually worse than the OEM rotor and pad. The pad material had chunks and voids in it, and when I saw its effect on my new rotor, I took it off and ordered a set of DP pads. Next problem.

The pad material on the pads were affixed to the back of the plate, mirror image style, so I had to return them for exchange. The exchange set had the pads on the correct side, but they were 20% undersized, reducing the contact surface area.

I just gave up at that point and went ahead and put them on, since they were at least useable.

It just speaks of the iffy quality control of the aftermarket pads.

When I need my next set, it'll be OEM pads for sure. The only advantage the aftermaket has is price, but there's too much given up to make it work the savings.
