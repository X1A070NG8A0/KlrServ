# Doohickey Adjustment??

## Post 1 — Browndog, 2009-04-11
<https://www.klr650.net/forums/forum/klr650-forums-klr650-net/klr650-maintenance-klr-wrenching-modifications-technical-support/62378-doohickey-adjustment#post62378>

Hey guys,I have a question concerning the doohickey,I have an 06 and in the owners manual on pg.72 there is a heading called Balancer Chain.This is what it states-Wear of the balancer chain and the chain guide develops slack between them. Adjust the balancer chain tensioner in accordance with the Periodic Maintenance Chart.It's 500mi. for the first adjustment,3000mi for the 2nd, and thereafter every 3000mi. Next up is a CAUTION- If the balancer chain is left unadjusted,the developed slack will make noise and may result in engine damage. CHAIN TENSIONER ADJUSTMENT-Remove the tensioner cap and loosen the tensioner bolt a few turns.Tighten back the bolt and install the cap in the original position.Has anybody ever made these adjustments at the time called for?Do you think the slack that occurs will make the spring fail due to excessive vibration?If these adjustments were made will it prolong the life of the doohicky?Last question-This goes out to all the people who had their doohicky fail-Did you ever adjust your balancer chain using this procedure?Thanks for your time...

Re: Doohickey Adjustment??


The conventional wisdom seems to be that the instructions in the manual are wrong and if you loosen the bolt as much as called for the doo is likely to fall off or become tilted and jammed. The link below describes what is surely a better way to do it. Unforturnately this link talks a lot about a sight glass which you would have to install. But they key seems to be to lean the bike over and just loosen the bolt one half turn and than tap it a little.

http://www.multisurfacemotorcycling.com/klrpage/leveradj/leveradj_p1.htm

The only time you should loosen that adjustment bolt is when you remove it to replace your stock Doohickey with an EM upgrade...IMHO...and then make your adjustments per the book after that.

I did mine and helped three friends a couple weeks ago. Two out of the four were broken and my bike was fine even though I've got more miles than all three bikes combined and I'm positive it has never been adjusted as there is only one mark from the bolt on the stock Doo.

One of the broken Doo's left a piece in the bottom of the motor we had to fish out and the other would have done the same if the adjustment bolt had ever been loosened.

My $0.02
