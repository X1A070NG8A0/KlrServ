Re: Air Filter Oil?


The K&N filter oil will work but it's not the best for a foam filter. I have used it in the past before I bought some foam filter oil. If you use it spray the filter and then work the oil into the foam by squeezing the filter in your hand. Wear some plastic gloves if you must. The K&N oil tends to be to thin in viscosity for the foam filter. I was lucky enough to be able to pickup a used K&N from someone recently. The stuff I bought is made by pj and they sell it as a set cleaner & oil together. I really liked the oil as it become tacky after 15-20 mins so it really traps the dirt. It also makes handling the filter for reinstallation a simpler task.
