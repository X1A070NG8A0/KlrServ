My uncle is a motorcycle mechanic, has been for decades.

When my old KLR a 1999 needed its valves adjusted he did them for me.

I watched him, and he did not unbolt the cams etc. as many do.

He had a little tool that pried the sprind down and allowed him to slip the shims in and out.

It literally took him about 2 minutes to actually check the gap and adjust the valves.

The part that took the time was moving the junk out of the way, and getting the covers off, and then putting it all back together.

That took him more like 40minutes.


I watched how fast he was and was humbled by his speed and skill.

Made me almost want to walk away and give up ever picking up a tool again to see how poor of a wrench turner I am in comparison to him.


But that little compressor gizmo was slick as heck.


I checked the valves myself on my 2008, and they are in spec.

But if they get out and need adjusting, I will borrow his tool, and make the job a lot easier on myself.

The tool doesn't work on the l/h side. It's far easier to unbolt the cam bearings and lift the camshafts enough to pluck the shim. It's not an issue, and nothing has changed since then.
