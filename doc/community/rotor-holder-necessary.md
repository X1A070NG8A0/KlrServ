Re: Removing flywheel: is the rotor holder really necessary?


You ask: "Is it absolutely necessary?" Of course not.

I have removed the rotor more than once, but I used the rotor holder. One reason why I use the rotor holder is because I don't like the alternative, which is using an impact wrench.

Why?

Because in my mind there is a chance that the repeated high-impact may loosen the magnets that are merely glued to the rotor. I also don't like the impact beating the crap out of my rod bearings.

Notwithstanding my concerns, I know that many folks have used a big honking impact to remove rotors on other motorcycle engines with seemingly no ill effects. But the risk is not worth it to me.

Re: Removing flywheel: is the rotor holder really necessary?


I have made two rotor holders (one for me and one for a friend). I use a $10 32mm open end wrench from Harbor Freight. Just heat the handle and bend it out and back in to make about a 1/2" offset to get around the rotor lip. It takes five minutes. If you don't have a torch, take it to an independent muffler shop. They might do it for for a couple of bucks or free.


Works fine for both removal and reinstalling/torquing the rotor bolt.


You can make a puller tool using a bolt, but my vendor wanted more for the bolt than Eager Mike does for his puller.
