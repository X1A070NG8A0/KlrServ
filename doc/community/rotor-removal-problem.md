> Too late to find out what you are doing wrong. EM's special puller is your only likely fix. Granted I'm just a backyard wantabe mechanic, in my experiences an impact is best method for removing a rotor. That and be sure and lube the threaded puller.

Yes, it is too late this time. But, for others in the future, I find that setting the impact wrench at its minimum torque setting gives lots of small blows which help jar the rotor loose. Also, rather than stripping the threads in the rotor or the puller if the rotor doesn't come off when the puller is good and snug, a couple of taps with a hammer on the end of the puller can jar the rotor loose.


Some say the jarring will harm the magnets in the rotor. On the four I have done the charging systems are still working fine.
