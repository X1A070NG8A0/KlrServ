Re: &gt;&gt;Dumb question, but what spark plug do you recommend?


I have an iridium plug sitting amongst many in my too chest. I use NGK stock ones. $3.00 a pop you cant beat that. I also take my bike apart very often and will use plug checks for *some* information when tuning my carb. As such I usually just go to local dealer and order up a handful.


From what I gather the iridium isnt "better" in the sense that performance will be better- it pretty much the same exact thing- just made of a harder metal. Each spark wears a little on the plug. A harder material wears less for a longer duration. If you go in there every 5 k stock is fine. If you dont like digging in there iridium is a good choice for a long time having not to worry about it.


Either way, we're talking about the difference of $5.00 so its not like its a major problem go for an iridium if you dont need it- or even a problem at all
