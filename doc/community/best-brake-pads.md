Re: Best brake pads?


I prefer the Galfer green for the front. When I first got my bike they were the ones most recommended here. I feel they stop better than stock and I get good mileage out of them. Stock were replaced at 6500 with some use left on them, Galfers went 13000, 10000, and the last set went 8000. On the rear, I used up the stock pads in about 12300 miles, then I burned through a set of Galfer blacks in 3700 miles, and a set of EBC organic FA pads in a little over 4000, then went to OEM and got about 9000 miles out of them, and 8000 out of the next set. Performance wise I can lock the rear just fine with stock pads. Looking back through my brake pad history I have progressively gone through them faster and faster. Apparently I brake a little more aggressively than I used to.
