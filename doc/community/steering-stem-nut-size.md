To straighten the front end alignment:

Loosen the front axle nut

Loosen all the triple clamps bolts until there is just a little drag - be careful, if you do it too much the front end can drop.

Either grab the front wheel between your legs and tug on the bars until straight - or find your ex-wifes boyfriends truck, hold the bars, and bang the side of the front tire on the truck until all is straight. Ex-girlfriends boyfriends truck works too. As does the side of a big tree trunk, or show your buddy how to do it on the side of his garage door jamb.


Then gently and properly tighten things up, and ride into the sunset with a smile..............


If you want to adjust the steering bearings too, then you need the big wrench.
