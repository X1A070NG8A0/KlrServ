Re: Finding oil filters


It can be difficult to get micron numbers from filter manu's. But they are rated in "Average" micron passing size. So if a filter ranges from 10 microns to 50 microns then it would be rated at 30 microns, but will pass some particles of 50 microns. The Scotts filter is rated at 35 microns "Absolute". This means that NOTHING larger than 35 will pass through. The average oil film clearing distance on mian bearings is 25 microns, making the Scotts seem ineffective or marginal. The "Average" rating on Scotts would be MUCH smaller than 35. BUT, the "Average" paper filter may do even worse. For more in depth info you can check this out: http://findarticles.com/p/articles/m...9/ai_n16120720

The FRAM filter for the KLR is OK. Avoid the FRAM spin-on types for your other bikes as they tend to have inadequate filter area.

It is a paper filter, just like wix, just like OEM. I would say AVOID THEM ALL, and buy HiFloFiltro, which uses a synthetic filter media. HiFloFiltro part # HF123

Best filter available.
