From the world famous http://www.bigcee.com/klr650faq.html#finaldrive

What kind of chain can I use?

Any quality, O-ring or X-ring chain should be fine, for example the DID 520 VM gold. The manual states that the chain must be an endless type; however, most people run a clip-type master with no problems. The size is 520 x 106 links. Incidentally, the BMW F650 FAQ chain page is an excellent resource.
