Re: Chain press/rivet tool... do I really need?

I think they make the job easier, but I got by without one on my last bike.

To remove a pin to break the chain, I just used a parallel pin punch to knock out one of the joining pins. Grinding the head back a little will make it a little easier to punch through. Get someone to hold a chunk of steel, like an axe head, beside the pin you want to knock out. Removing the rear wheel will give you the room you need.

Use the old chain to get the length you need to shorten the new one to and knock out a pin the same way.

Fit the new chain and install O rings between the side plates and push through a new pin. One end of the pin will be slightly deformed so it won’t slide through the hole, so all you need to do is install the pin, place your big chunk of steel against the back side and deform the good end so it won’t work its way loose. I did it by giving it a whack with a ball-pein hammer.

It's not really a one man job this way though.
