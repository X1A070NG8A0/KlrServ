Re: Best Chain?


Sanibel, You sure your chain is not running tight? 3K miles I would not expect to see any noticeable wear.


My recommendation is an OEM sprocket set if possible. The OEM rear sprocket is super durable. The front OEM sprocket has the rubber shoulder when helps quiet the chain. I like the Sun front sprocket as an alternative.


For the chain, I buy the EK SRO 520X106 chain. It's inexpensive and works very well. I also recommend the EK Screwstyle masterlink if you do not have a riveting tool. Nothing wrong with a clip-style masterlink either, if it is installed properly, and then safety wired for mental peace.


Running this combo, with infrequent care yields 13-15K miles.


The Key is to adjust your chain properly. Very easy to have the chain too tight, and most shops, including dealerships set the bike up wrong. This is a Dirt bike style Uni-track suspension with larger travel. They set the bike up for 2-3" of travel. Not good at all for the chain, sprockets, or the output shaft bushings and seals.
