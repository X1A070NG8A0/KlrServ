# Oil filter comparison: HF123 vs. KN123

## Post 1 — electroken, 2009-04-18
<https://www.klr650.net/forums/forum/klr650-forums-klr650-net/klr650-maintenance-klr-wrenching-modifications-technical-support/62767-oil-filter-comparison-hf123-vs-kn123#post62767>

I decided to see for myself why the K&N KN123 filter usually costs twice as much as the Hiflo Filtro HF123. Below are a couple of pictures with the K&N on the right for both.


What's the same? Almost everything. The filter media looks the same even at 7X magnification. The support tubes are identical. Even the plating on the end caps and the glue holding in the media appears to be the same. The boxes for both state "Made in Thailand". I'm betting they came off the same machinery.


What's different? The Hiflo Filtro has 37 pleats of media. The K&N has 46 pleats, so it has roughly 25% more media surface area than the HF123 has. Does it matter? Not unless you run your filters until they're crammed full of junk. Oh, and the K&N has a nicer box.


The cheap bastard in me will be buying only the HF123 in the future.
