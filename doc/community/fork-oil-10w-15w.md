## Post 2 — holycaveman, 2006-02-05
<https://www.klr650.net/forums/forum/klr650-forums-klr650-net/klr650-first-generation-forums/18205-10w-versus-15w-fork-oil?p=380317#post380317>

If you are bottoming out then I would go with 15-20wt oil. Stock I believe is comparable to about 7wt. 10 is not going to make a substantial difference in my op. If you have stiffer springs then a heavier oil would complement your setup by increasing the dampening because the heavier springs decrease the dampening on the rebound stroke.

## Post 5 — Meat Wad, 2006-02-05
<https://www.klr650.net/forums/forum/klr650-forums-klr650-net/klr650-first-generation-forums/18205-10w-versus-15w-fork-oil?p=380410#post380410>

160lbs is pretty light, i would use a 10 weight, remember just because it is off road doesnt mean you necessarily want a stiff suspension. Look at any dirt bike suspension it is plush and soft, not stiff. A heavier weight oil will slow down rebound and act sluggish in the dirt, not a great feature. I'll bet most of the guys running 15w or heavier are over 225lbs and that makes a big difference.
